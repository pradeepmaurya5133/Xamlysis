How It Works

From Sign Up To Score

Two short paths — one for students, one for teachers. Pick yours and follow the steps in order; each one takes a minute or two.

If You Are A Student

Five steps from a new account to a graded result.

  1. 1

    Create your account

    Register with your email address and pick the student role. Your email is your username everywhere in the portal.

    Create an account
  2. 2

    Choose your class

    Select the class you are in, from 1 to 12. Exams are scoped by class, so this decides which papers can reach you.

    Select a class
  3. 3

    Enrol in your courses

    Add each subject you study. An exam only appears when its course is one you are enrolled in and its class matches yours.

    Enrol in courses
  4. 4

    Register for an exam

    Open your scheduled exams and register for the ones you intend to sit. Do this before the exam date so the paper is ready for you.

    See scheduled exams
  5. 5

    Attempt and review

    On the day, start the exam from your dashboard. Submit when finished and your marks and percentage appear immediately — then review each answer against the correct one.

    See past results

If You Are A Teacher

Four steps from an empty schedule to a graded class.

  1. 1

    Create your account

    Register with your email address and pick the teacher role. You will land on a dashboard built around scheduling rather than attempting.

    Create an account
  2. 2

    Schedule the exam

    Set the code, title, date, start time, duration, question count and maximum marks, then attach it to a course and a class.

    Schedule an exam
  3. 3

    Author the question paper

    Straight after scheduling, add each question with four options, mark which one is correct, and give it a mark value. Your schedule shows Ready once the paper is complete.

    Start a paper
  4. 4

    Watch the results come in

    Track registrations before the day, then review attempts, averages, and the highest and lowest scores once the exam has run.

    View your timetable
