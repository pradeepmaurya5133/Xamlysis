Three Steps To A Graded Paper

The whole cycle, from an empty schedule to a result a student can learn from.

1

Schedule

A teacher sets the date, duration and marks, attaches the exam to a course and a class, then writes the questions. Students in that class see it appear.

2

Attempt

Students register ahead of the day and sit the paper under its timer. Question order is shuffled for every attempt.

3

Review

Marks and percentage are calculated on submit. Students compare their answers against the correct ones; teachers get class averages and spread.

Common Questions

The things people ask before they start.

What do I use as my username?

Your email address — Xamlysis keys accounts on email rather than a separate username.

Why can I not see any exams?

An exam reaches you only when its class matches yours and you are enrolled in its course. Check both in Classes and Courses.

Is there negative marking?

No. A wrong answer scores zero for that question; nothing is deducted from your total.

When do I get my result?

Immediately on submit, along with a question-by-question breakdown of what you got right.
