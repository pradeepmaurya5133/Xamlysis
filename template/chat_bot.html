Help & Support

Common questions about using Xamlysis, and where to go for anything else.

How do I register for an exam?

Open Scheduled Exams and press Register on the exam you want. You must be in the right class and enrolled in the course for it to appear.

Why can't I see any exams?

Check that you have selected your class and enrolled in your courses. Exams are only shown to students whose class and course both match.

How is my score calculated?

Each question carries its own marks. Your total is the sum of the marks for correct answers, and the percentage is that total against the exam's maximum.

Can I review my answers?

Yes. On Previous Exams, press View on any completed exam to see each question with your answer and the correct one.

How do I schedule an exam?

Teachers can create an exam from Create Exam, then add questions through Question Setup until the paper is complete.

Something else?

Send your question straight to the teacher through Raise A Query, or update your contact details in Settings.
