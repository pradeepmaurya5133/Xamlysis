FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to what people ask most. If yours is not here, the contact page will reach us.

Accounts

What do I use as my username?

Your email address. Xamlysis keys accounts on email rather than a separate username, so that is what you enter on the login page.

Can one account be both a student and a teacher?

No. Each account holds a single role, fixed when you register. If you need both, register two accounts with different email addresses.

How do I change my mobile number?

Open Settings from your dashboard and update it there. It is the one profile field editable from the portal itself.

Exams

Why can I not see any exams?

An exam reaches you only when two things match: the class on the exam equals your class, and you are enrolled in the exam's course. Check both in Classes and Courses.

Do I have to register before an exam?

Yes. Registering tells the portal to prepare the paper for you. Register from your scheduled exams list any time before the exam date.

What happens if I run out of time?

The attempt carries the duration set by your teacher. Answer what you can within it — unanswered questions simply score zero rather than invalidating the attempt.

Are the questions always in the same order?

No. Question order is shuffled for each attempt, so two students sitting side by side will not see the same sequence.

Scoring

How is my score calculated?

Every question carries its own marks. Your total is the sum of the marks for the questions you answered correctly, and your percentage is that total against the exam's maximum.

Is there negative marking?

No. A wrong answer scores zero for that question; nothing is deducted from your total.

When do I get my result?

Immediately. Marks and percentage are calculated the moment you submit, and the exam moves into your previous exams list.

Can I see which questions I got wrong?

Yes. On Previous Exams, press View on any completed exam to see each question with your answer beside the correct one.

Teaching

How do I know a paper is finished?

Your upcoming exams list shows Ready once the number of authored questions reaches the question count you set, and Incomplete until then.

Can I see who registered?

Yes. The upcoming exams view shows a registration count per exam, and your dashboard lists the individual students for an exam you select.

How do students ask me something about an exam?

Through Raise A Query. The query is stored against the exam it was raised from, so the context travels with it.
